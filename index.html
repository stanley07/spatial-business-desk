Interactive Experience

The Hands-First Retail Workflow

Experience a deliberate 5-stage spatial transaction lifecycle designed for physical intuition, operator speed, and complete inventory accuracy.

STEP 01
See Your Inventory

Step into a 3D showroom where products live on physical pedestals with live stock counters and transparent Naira pricing.

STEP 02
Select with Your Hands

Reach toward products using natural hand rays or direct touch. Verified end-to-end with simulated XR hands in WebXR.

STEP 03
Prepare an Order

Items stage into the central sales basket. Critical rule: Stock is NOT deducted until explicit confirmation.

STEP 04
Confirm with Confidence

Target the ergonomically angled confirmation button. Debounce locks prevent accidental double-billing.

STEP 05

Watch inventory decrement, total revenue update, completed sales counter advance, and order reset instantaneously.

Vision & Philosophy

Why Spatial Business?

Flat spreadsheets and cluttered point-of-sale screens lack physical intuition and create room for costly bookkeeping errors. Spatial computing invites us to rethink operations as a physical workspace.

Verified In Current Prototype

Hands-First Commerce Prototype

A fully operational WebXR application demonstrating tangible spatial point-of-sale interaction:

  • Interactive 3D Pedestals: Procedural geometry and PBR materials rendered in Three.js r185.
  • Hands-First Interaction: Dual pointer model supporting both ray targeting and direct poke gestures.
  • Human Confirmation Safeguard: Explicit transaction gating preventing premature stock deduction.
  • Debounced Confirmation Engine: Multi-event deduplication preventing duplicate transactions.
  • Universal WebXR Deployment: Runs in Meta Quest Browser, desktop Chromium, and mobile browsers with zero install.
  • Sample In-Memory Ledger: Real-time in-memory inventory counters and revenue calculation.
Future Product Roadmap

Spatial Business Operating System

Architecture

Built for the Open Immersive Web

Engineered with Meta’s official developer tooling, high-performance WebGL rendering, and zero-allocation entity systems.

IW
Meta IWSDK v1.1.0

Meta Immersive Web SDK driving WebXR session management, camera framing, and input pointer routing.

XR
Open WebXR Standards

Cross-platform browser standard enabling immediate immersive execution across Meta Quest and desktop.

3D
Three.js r185

High-performance 3D scene graph, procedural geometry, custom lighting gradients, and contact shadows.

EC
EliCS v3.4.2 ECS

Pure entity-component architecture with query subscriptions and zero per-frame garbage collection allocations.

TS
TypeScript + Vite

Strict type safety, modern modular architecture, and rapid build times for edge deployment.

CF
Cloudflare Pages

Instant global edge delivery over HTTPS, optimized for sub-second asset loading on mobile and headsets.
